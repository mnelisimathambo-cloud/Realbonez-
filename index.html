FAQ

Common questions.

What information do you need for a quote?

Vehicle make, model, year, VIN or registration, whether you have a working key, your location and clear photos of the key or module label.

Do you offer mobile service?

Yes, for supported jobs and selected KwaZulu-Natal service areas. Travel outside the normal area is quoted separately.

Do you supply genuine OEM keys?

Yes, where available. Genuine OEM keys are quoted separately because pricing and availability vary by vehicle.
